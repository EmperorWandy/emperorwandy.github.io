31-Day Reset

A daily fitness and wellness coach by Akinwande Akinkunmi Adeniyi

31-Day Reset tells you what to do each day for 31 days, shows you how to do it, and reminds you so you actually do it.

Open 31-Day Reset Get the Android app

What the app does

  • Five goals, each with its own fixed 31-day plan: General Fitness, Fat Loss, Healthy Weight Gain, Heart and Stamina, and Gentle Start.
  • Two tasks a day: a guided workout with timers, pictures of every exercise and a Nigerian voice coach, and an evening wellness habit.
  • Reminders 30 minutes before your workout and at your evening time.
  • A food and water log with Nigerian foods and a simple plate check.
  • Progress charts, badges, a forgiving streak and a certificate on Day 31.
  • On Android: a home screen widget, step counting and Health Connect.

Your data and Google

Your progress is stored on your own phone. Nobody else can see it, including the app's author.

Google Drive backup (optional). If you choose to connect Google Drive, 31-Day Reset asks Google for permission to see and change only the files it creates. It saves one file, "31-Day Reset backup.json", in your own Google Drive, so you can bring your progress back on a new phone. It cannot see any other files in your Drive, and the file is never sent to the author or anyone else.

Health notice

31-Day Reset gives general fitness and wellness guidance only. It is not medical advice. Speak to a doctor before starting if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, a recent operation or injury, or are pregnant.
